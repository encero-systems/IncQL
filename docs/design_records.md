<h2>IncQL RFCs</h2>
<p><a href="../rfcs/">Requests for Comments</a> define IncQL's stable design intent and record why important boundaries exist. They cover the language and carrier foundations, Prism and Substrait, Session execution, the function catalog, evidence and governance, external ingress, and backend modes.</p>
<p>RFCs are moment-in-time design records. Use the <a href="../language/reference/">Reference</a> pages for current package behavior and the RFC series when you need the normative contract, rationale, alternatives, or design history.</p>
